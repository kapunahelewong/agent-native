## How it works

Content lives in `.mdx` files under the `content/` directory. The navigation is configured in `docs.config.json`. Both are editable by the agent or directly in your editor.

<Note>
  You don't need to rebuild the app when you edit content. Pages are compiled on-the-fly from the MDX files.
</Note>
